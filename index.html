and simply become a memory.

And then there are people like you —
the ones who become a feeling.

You're crazy enough to make conversations unforgettable,
funny enough to make ordinary moments better,
open-minded enough to understand without judging,
and strong enough to chase what you want with dedication.

I admire the way you respect your parents,
the values you carry,
and the person you're becoming.

And now, I have one last prediction for you...

I don't know if someone has ever told you this,
and honestly, maybe you have no taste if they haven't —
but I'll tell you anyway.

You're beautiful now.
